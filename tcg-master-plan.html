Cards per deck

Eight exact starter decks. A new player chooses one starter initially.

6

Reward Cards

Starting hand is 7. Deck, hand, discard, Reward Cards, Vanguard and Reserve are core battle zones.

Creature progression

  • Primary evolution chain: Baby → Teen → Adult.
  • Only one evolution per creature stack per turn.
  • Printed HP minimum 40; printed HP maximum 390.
  • HP may exceed 390 only through temporary or conditional effects.
  • Creature design must remain species-diverse; the Stream Bandit stag is the master brand identity, not the dominant creature species.
  • Mythic remains a special copy-limit class. Existing Standalone designs must be audited against the final current-rules role before being retained.

Turn and play rules

  • Every playable card is played from the player's hand; Essence is a real card, not a detached external resource system.
  • First player cannot attack on their first personal turn.
  • First-player Ally restriction remains part of the rules.
  • Vanguard / Reserve movement, withdrawal, evolution, attacks and Aftermath are server-authoritative.
  • Shield and conditions are first-class battle mechanics.

Economy and progression

Exactly three currencies

  1. Battle Pass Tokens
  2. Trade Tokens
  3. Shop Coins

Do not introduce substitute or duplicate currency systems.

Collection limits

  • Normal card: keep up to 4 copies; extras convert under the economy rules.
  • Mythic: keep up to 1 copy.
  • Collection, Deck Builder, Trading, Shop, Battle Pass, Sets/Series and card presentation remain planned product layers.

Modes

Arcade is the current non-ranked progression mode. Ranked matchmaking, player XP/rank and battle rewards are master-plan systems. Private friend/test rooms remain useful for testing and direct battles and are not the normal reward-eligible matchmaking path.

Card redesign rule

The first-release card text is not authoritative. Names, creatures, themes, elemental identity and strong concepts may be retained, but costs, HP, attacks, abilities, timing and effects must be changed wherever the current master rules or game balance require it.

REWRITEEffect conflicts with the current game or creates bad gameplay and must be redesigned.

Implementation ledger

Completed / branch-proven foundations

  • TCG source recovery and additive foundation/security migrations.
  • Atomic one-starter grant and server deck validation.
  • Private room lobby, join-code compatibility and match state shell.
  • Atomic match command commit path.
  • Card printing/art bucket source migration.
  • Three-currency and copy-limit alignment source migration.
  • Automatic matchmaking and Arcade progression/rewards source migration.
  • tcg-match-actions is the intended sole in-battle turn/Aftermath owner.

Implemented but subject to card rewrite

  • tcg-tactic-actions currently covers all 40 structured Ally/Device definitions in the old registry.
  • Six lifecycle-sensitive old definitions have shared hooks for temporary attack bonus, withdrawal cost, condition immunity, final-Vanguard attack eligibility and generated Essence cleanup.
  • This runtime coverage is useful engineering, but it does not make those old card designs final.

Known architecture debt / blockers

  • Attack resolution still parses printed English text. This must be replaced after the card redesign.
  • Three current old attacks explicitly need a pending-choice engine: Celestyr — Dream Ray, Nebulynx — Starfall Path and Myceliarch — Mycelial Bloom.
  • Creature abilities still need a deterministic structured registry/interpreter pass after their card designs are audited.
  • The legacy t.html Arcade Lab is a useful branch test harness, not the final production route.
