Can the game be fully ready today?

What can be ready immediately

The master-plan/control surface can stay live independently, and safe branch-only TCG ticks can continue one at a time with exact evidence.

What should not be promised today

A fully production-ready TCG. The remaining work includes substantial runtime event/choice/listener work, the final player-facing application, deployment gates and a genuine two-account complete battle. Those are engineering/release gates, not cosmetic polish.

Practical target: first reach a boring, deterministic, complete two-player private-alpha battle. Collection, shop, ranked, Battle Pass polish and public SEO can then be layered without risking the battle engine.
