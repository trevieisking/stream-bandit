# Stream Bandit TCG — Special Mechanic Rule Matrix V1.1

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Purpose:** Post-test consistency layer for damage affinity, card-header truth and the supplied special-card rule examples.

## Damage-affinity rule family

The supplied historical/current card examples visibly show weakness/resistance-style rules, but Stream Bandit already has a canonical ownership split that must be preserved.

| Visible rule pattern | Stream Bandit presentation | Server requirement | Canonical source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary element/type matchup increases attack damage | may be displayed as **Vulnerability** with clear accessible explanation | derive attacker/defender element/type keys, evaluate versioned matchup snapshot, apply current ordinary 2x rule at most once | global `matchup_version` / `cp2-matchups-v0.1` |
| exceptional card reduces/prevents attack damage from configured element/type/tag | clearly labelled **Resistance** rule on the card | apply explicit structured exceptional resistance in canonical damage order | card-specific structured data |
| exceptional card changes its normal matchup behaviour | explicit matchup/form rule text | apply reviewed structured matchup override without changing the global table for other cards | card-specific `matchup_override` |

### Ordinary Vulnerability is not per-card Weakness data

For ordinary Set One-style cards:

- do **not** add per-card `weakness` objects;
- ordinary matchup strength/weakness belongs to the versioned match rules snapshot;
- attacker and defender element/type keys are evaluated by the resolver;
- current Weakness/Vulnerability applies `2x` once maximum per attack;
- non-attack damage does not automatically use that multiplier.

The player-facing term can be refined later without moving engine ownership away from the global matchup table.

### Exceptional Resistance remains card data

Resistance is not generated by the ordinary matchup chain. A future card may explicitly define resistance or a matchup override when its design requires it.

### Separation rules

Damage affinity remains independent from:

- rarity;
- printing finish;
- special Creature class;
- Reward value;
- ordinary Shield;
- Condition state;
- position-based prevention.

A Shine/Alt-Art printing has exactly the same gameplay affinity/matchup behaviour as the same gameplay identity's Standard printing.

### Damage counters are separate

Ordinary Vulnerability/Weakness, Resistance and Shield do not automatically modify:

- placed damage counters;
- moved existing damage counters;
- turn-transition Condition damage counters.

Any exception must be explicit structured rules data.

## Card-header consistency note

The final showcase families remain the visual composition reference, but concept-art Cost numbers do not create rules.

Current ordinary `sb-tcg-card-v0.2` Creatures have no generic play/evolution Cost field.

Therefore `tcg-card-visual-printing-v1.1.json` requires:

- top-left badge slot may show a real structured header property or nonnumeric class/stage/special-rule badge;
- numeric Cost only when the schema truly defines one;
- Element/type top-right;
- name/stage plus clearly labelled HP in the upper identity/header area;
- large artwork;
- exactly two ordinary Creature action slots;
- Withdraw Cost bottom-right.

## Special-family naming note

External research names are provenance only. Stream Bandit player-facing working names are defined by `tcg-special-mechanic-names-v1.json` and must compose generic capabilities rather than create one engine per label.

## Acceptance

V1.1 now preserves:

- global matchup ownership for ordinary Vulnerability;
- exceptional structured Resistance/overrides;
- schema-honest card presentation;
- semantic separation for damage counters;
- original Stream Bandit special-family naming.
