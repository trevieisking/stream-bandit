# Stream Bandit TCG V2.4.40 Ledger — Deck Accessories

## Identity
Official deck accessory identity is independent from gameplay card printing identity.

- `starter_id` → official deck product identity.
- `bundle_id` → matching three-piece accessory bundle.
- `accessory_id` → exact collectible Sleeve / Battle Coin / Deck Box identity.
- `artwork_id` → exact media revision for that accessory.

## Launch baseline
Eight active starter products × three accessories = 24 launch accessory slots.

## Included-with-deck rule
Acquiring an official deck ensures its matching three cosmetics once. Existing owners require safe backfill when implemented.

## Shop rule
Every unowned themed accessory must be obtainable through the in-game Shop after the canonical economy/catalog owner exists. Owned items are not sold as accidental duplicates.

## Safety
Battle Coins are cosmetic only and never influence server toss/randomness. Sleeves do not leak hidden card identity. Deck Boxes do not modify legality/capacity.

## Future content
All future promoted official starter/deck products inherit the same rule. Future concept planning does not change the eight-deck runtime baseline.
