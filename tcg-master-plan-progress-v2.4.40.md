# Stream Bandit TCG Progress V2.4.40 — Deck Accessories & Shop

The product requirement is now locked: every official starter/deck product includes a matching sleeve/card-back set, cosmetic battle coin and deck box.

The current eight active starter IDs are sourced directly from `tcg-set-one-starters-v0.2.json`. Their 24 accessory identities are recorded in `assets/tcg/accessories/tcg-deck-accessory-ledger-v1.json`.

The Shop page now visibly reserves Card Sleeves, Battle Coins, Deck Boxes and Accessory Bundles while still refusing to invent prices or purchase grants before the canonical economy owner exists.

No gameplay rule, deck recipe, pack odds, currency balance, database schema or Supabase deployment is changed by this checkpoint.

Next implementation remains gated: artwork creation, accessory ownership/backfill owner, deck cosmetic selection and owner-backed Shop catalog/grants.
